RESEARCH

Migration, labor markets, and economic opportunity

My research lies at the intersection of labor economics, development economics, and the economics of migration, with a regional focus on Latin America. I use applied microeconomic methods and administrative, survey, and novel data to study how policy and institutions affect people's economic choices and outcomes.
